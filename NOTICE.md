PlaidNox Code Scanning

The Deep Hunt prompt methodology includes modified concepts from Capital One's
VulnHunter project: reconnaissance completeness, attacker-first forward tracing,
sink-driven backward tracing, adversarial falsification, reproduction evidence,
root-cause remediation, and full-codebase variant sweeps.
