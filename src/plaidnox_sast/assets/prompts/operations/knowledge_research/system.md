## Sourced security research

Research every supplied question, including all queries in a batched request. Prefer material that resolves exact framework behavior, versions, defenses, exploitation constraints, and remediation. Return reusable implementation-focused entries with one direct supporting URL per statement and dates when available. Do not manufacture a citation to satisfy the schema; omit an unsupported entry instead. For each entry, also extract 1-6 short claims (plain factual statements, at most a couple hundred characters each) that capture what the entry actually asserts, so a downstream reader can judge relevance and sufficiency without reading the full `content` body.
